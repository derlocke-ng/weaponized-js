Peer-to-peer webtools, armed with nostr.

Every tool here runs entirely in your browser and syncs over relays you choose. No sign-up needed, no server of ours, no tracking — and encryption happens before anything leaves your device.

Loadout

Grocery lists, to-dos, household inventory and markdown notes that sync between all your devices and whoever you share them with — rentry-style links, end-to-end encrypted. Works offline, and your devices put back whatever the relays forget.

Relays right now

Loadout syncs over these nostr relays; pick your own in its settings. Payload and pongjs still signal through public gun relays until they move to nostr too. Relays pass data along; they can’t read anything that was encrypted first.
