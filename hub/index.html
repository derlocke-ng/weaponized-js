weaponized.js Source

Peer-to-peer webtools, armed with gun.js.

Every tool here runs entirely in your browser and syncs over public gun relays. No accounts to make, no server of ours, no tracking — and encryption happens before anything leaves your device.

New

Loadout

Grocery lists, to-dos, household inventory and markdown notes that sync between all your devices and whoever you share them with — rentry-style links, end-to-end encrypted. Works offline, and your devices put back whatever the relays forget.

  • Payload

    Send files straight to another browser while you’re both online. Direct WebRTC when possible, encrypted through gun otherwise, every 64 KB piece checked with SHA-256.

    • WebRTC

    Retro two-player Pong between two browsers. Share a link or QR code or pick an open game, then play over a direct connection.
